The test comes with no obligations. It is the regular, complete Lampada: use it as you normally would, free and without ads. When the app is released to everyone, it simply updates through Google Play, and you can leave the test at any time on the invitation page.

## How to join

It is easiest to follow these steps on your Android phone. Only the group's administrators can see your email address.

1. Join the [testers group](https://groups.google.com/g/lampada-testers) (with the same Google account you use for Google Play on your phone) with the “Join group” button.
2. Open the [test invitation](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/app.lampada) and tap “Become a tester”.
3. Install [Lampada from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.lampada). The link starts working a few minutes after step 2.

## Why it matters

Google releases Lampada to everyone only after at least 12 testers have used it for 14 days in a row. So it helps us a lot if you keep it installed until the test ends and open it every couple of days: pray, listen to a passage, write a journal entry.

We would love to hear any impressions: what you liked, what felt awkward, what is missing. Write to us through [support](/support) or leave feedback in Google Play — during the test only we can see it.
