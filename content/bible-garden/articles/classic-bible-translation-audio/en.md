## Why some KJV words are hard to follow today

The KJV was first published in 1611. Its Early Modern English gives it a recognizable rhythm, but *thee*, *thou*, and endings such as *-eth* are no longer everyday English. Other words still look familiar but have changed meaning: *suffer* can mean “allow,” *prevent* means “go before,” and *charity* means “love.” Hearing them without seeing the sentence can make the meaning harder to catch.

Here are short excerpts from the [KJV text on eBible.org](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/), alongside WEB and BSB texts used in Bible Garden. **KJV is not in the app**; the WEB column is the US edition.

| Passage | KJV — not in the app | WEB | BSB |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mark 10:14 | “Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not” | “Allow the little children to come to me! Don’t forbid them” | “Let the little children come to Me, and do not hinder them!” |
| 1 Thessalonians 4:15 | “shall not prevent them which are asleep” | “will in no way precede those who have fallen asleep” | “will by no means precede those who have fallen asleep” |
| 1 Corinthians 13:4 | “Charity suffereth long, and is kind; charity envieth not” | “Love is patient and is kind. Love doesn’t envy.” | “Love is patient, love is kind. It does not envy” |
| Matthew 6:25 | “Take no thought for your life” | “don’t be anxious for your life” | “do not worry about your life” |

“Take no thought” here means “do not worry,” rather than “never think about it.” A modern translation can make that distinction clear without requiring you to give up the KJV you know.

## When another source is the better fit

- **You specifically want KJV:** use the KJV sources at the top of this page. Bible Garden offers WEB and BSB instead.
- **You want downloads or offline audio:** use the downloadable KJV recordings from LibriVox, AudioTreasure, or Public Domain Audio Bibles. Bible Garden streams audio and has no audio downloads.
- **You want dramatization or music:** choose a suitable KJV recording elsewhere; all English recordings in Bible Garden are voice only.
- **You use Android or want a browser player:** Bible Garden currently runs on iPhone and iPad. An Android version and listening on our website are in our plans.
- **You want books beyond the 66-book canon:** the app does not include deuterocanonical books.

We hand-pick each text and recording for quality, so our selection is small.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Where can I listen to the KJV audio Bible free?

YouVersion offers all 66 books read by Max McLean. Bible Gateway offers several KJV recordings in its browser player. LibriVox and AudioTreasure also provide free recordings, including downloadable files.

### Can I download the KJV audio Bible for offline listening?

Yes. LibriVox’s complete recording by Michael Armenta is available through Internet Archive, and AudioTreasure offers Dan Wagner’s Old and New Testament MP3s. Public Domain Audio Bibles offers a text-to-speech recording in ZIP files. Bible Garden does not download audio for offline use.

### Is the King James Version in Bible Garden?

No. The English translations in the app are the World English Bible, its British Edition, and the Berean Standard Bible, each with audio for all 66 books.

### Is WEB an updated KJV?

WEB belongs to the same translation lineage, through the Revised Version and the American Standard Version of 1901. It is a modern-English revision of the ASV, rather than a direct update of the KJV.

### Which audio Bible in the app has no music?

All four English recordings: WEB read by Winfred Henson, the uncredited WEB British Edition recording, and BSB read by Bob Souer or David.

### Can I hear KJV and WEB verse by verse in Multi Reading?

No, because KJV is not in the app. You can pair WEB with BSB and hear each verse in both translations, with a pause between them and both texts visible.
