## A free Bible text does not make every recording free
<!-- screen: reader-picker-english -->

The BSB text is public domain, as its publisher explains on its [licensing page](https://berean.bible/licensing.htm); so is the [WEB text](https://ebible.org/eng-web/copyright.htm). But text and audio have separate rights. Text rights generally expire decades after a translator’s death, while a narrator and recording producer can have rights lasting many years after a recording is made. An old translation can have a new, protected recording.

A download is straightforward when:

1. **The rights holder releases the recording for everyone to use**, through a public-domain dedication or an open license. You can copy and share it under the stated terms; some licenses require attribution.
2. **An authorized site offers a download under its own terms.** Those terms may allow personal listening or noncommercial ministry use without allowing redistribution.

This guide reports what publishers and download sites say about their recordings; it is not legal advice.

## How to spot a questionable download

Be cautious when:

- the page names no publisher, rights holder, or terms of use;
- a torrent or file-sharing archive has no link to the original source;
- someone offers a free copy of a recording the publisher sells or only streams;
- a generic “Audio Bible” app contains ads but never identifies its recordings.

A ZIP file or file host is not inherently suspicious: legitimate publishers use them too. What matters is a traceable source and a clear statement of permission. If you are unsure, check the narrator’s or publisher’s own site.

## When you really need an MP3 file

Downloading makes sense when:

- **you will be without internet for days**, on a camping trip or a long journey;
- **your player cannot run an app**, such as a car stereo that takes a USB drive or an older MP3 player;
- **you want to give someone a recording on a drive or disc**;
- **your mobile connection is slow or expensive**.

Allow enough storage. In Bible Garden, Souer’s BSB runs about 69 hours and Henson’s WEB about 84 hours, measured from the app’s audio files on October 4, 2026. Archive size depends on duration and MP3 quality: Henson’s [WEB download](https://publicdomainaudiobibles.com/WEBW.html) lists 995 MB for the Old Testament and 320 MB for the New Testament — roughly 1.3 GB together. ### Where can I download the Bible text rather than audio?

The BSB publisher provides text downloads through its [licensing page](https://berean.bible/licensing.htm), and [eBible.org](https://ebible.org/eng-web/) provides the public-domain WEB text in downloadable formats. Text permissions and recording permissions are separate.

### Can I upload a downloaded recording to my YouTube channel?

Only when its permissions allow redistribution. Souer’s CC0 BSB and Henson’s public-domain WEB are clear examples. A personal-use download does not give you permission to re-upload it. Follow any attribution or other license conditions, even when the underlying Bible text is free.
