Looking for a free audio Bible app? The right choice depends on how you listen. These apps and services each do something different:

- **YouVersion** — for the widest choice of Bible translations and languages, reading plans, and an app on either major phone platform;
- **Dwell** — for a listening-first experience with a choice of voices, optional background music, and offline downloads; its full library requires a subscription;
- **Bible.is** — for free dramatized recordings in many languages and audio you can download;
- **The Bible in a Year** — for daily Scripture readings, prayer, and commentary with Fr. Mike Schmitz;
- **Bible Gateway** — for a large Bible library and audio you can play in a browser;
- **Bible Garden** — for hearing the same verse in multiple translations or languages, seeing all their texts together, pausing between passages, and moving precisely by verse.

Bible Garden is our app. We compared the services using their official app listings, help pages, and websites as checked on September 28, 2026; each has strengths ours does not. Sources: [YouVersion](https://help.youversion.com/l/en/article/2z77z3edpw-audio-bible-ios), [Dwell](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1343917374), [Bible.is](https://www.faithcomesbyhearing.com/audio-bible-resources/bible-is), [The Bible in a Year](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-bible-in-a-year-with-fr-mike-schmitz/id1539568321), [Bible Gateway](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id506512797).

## How we compared them

For an app you might listen to every day, we looked at:

- **Cost:** fully free, free with ads, or limited free access followed by a subscription;
- **Translations and voices:** what you can actually hear, rather than only read;
- **Offline listening:** whether audio can be downloaded;
- **Where it works:** iPhone, iPad, Android, or a browser;
- **What makes it useful:** a feature worth choosing it for.

This is a selection of well-known options with distinct uses, not a ranking of every Bible app.

## A closer look

### YouVersion

YouVersion is by far the most popular choice here, with millions of App Store ratings and more than 100 million Android installs. Its greatest strength is reach: thousands of Bible versions in more than 2,000 languages, plus thousands of reading plans and ways to read with friends. Selected versions have audio, with playback speed and timer controls. It is free and has no ads. If you want an NIV, ESV, KJV, or NLT recording, or a plan to follow, start here. Offline audio in the main app was not established by the sources used for this article.

### Dwell

Dwell is built around listening. You can choose from more than 20 voices and 14 translations, add background music, follow the text, and download audio for offline use. A limited selection is free; the full experience needs a subscription after a trial. It is a good choice if the sound of the reading matters most to you and you are comfortable paying for more voices and content.

### Bible.is

Bible.is, from Faith Comes By Hearing, offers free, ad-free audio in more than 1,700 languages. Some recordings are dramatized with multiple voices, music, and effects. You can download a version for offline listening. Choose it if you want that style of production or need a language that smaller libraries do not carry.

### The Bible in a Year

*The Bible in a Year (with Fr. Mike Schmitz)* is a free podcast from Ascension, not a general audio Bible app. Across 365 daily episodes, Fr. Mike reads Scripture, prays, and offers commentary. It follows the Catholic canon, including the deuterocanonical books. A podcast app can download episodes for offline listening. Choose it if you want a guided daily habit with explanation rather than a player for picking any chapter at any time. For a calendar you can follow at your own pace, see our [Bible in a year plan](/articles/bible-in-a-year/).

### Bible Gateway

Bible Gateway has a large library of translations and audio Bibles you can play in your browser, as well as an app. That makes it handy when you want to listen at a computer without installing anything. Free access includes ads; Bible Gateway Plus offers an ad-free experience. Check whether a particular translation has audio before settling on it.

Bible Garden also offers:

- **A separate speed for each Multi Reading step** (0.5×–2.5×) and a separate text size. Save a sequence as a template to use again.
- **Pauses in regular reading** after each verse, paragraph, or section, either for a set time or until you tap Play. You can make room to reflect without reaching for the pause button every time.
- **Exact verse-by-verse navigation.** Previous and next land at the start of a verse because the text and recording are aligned. In Multi Reading, you can also move between translations of the same verse.
- **A choice of narrators for some translations**, with a voice preview. The Berean Standard Bible includes Bob Souer and David; the World English Bible (US edition) is read by Winfred Henson. English audio covers all 66 books in these recordings.
- **Readable text** with section headings, cross references, adjustable type, and words of Jesus in color where the translation supplies that markup. Footnotes open on tap in regular reading.
- **Automatic progress tracking** as you listen through a chapter or read its text, with progress by book and across the Bible.
- **Regular playback controls:** adjustable speed, automatic next chapter, background audio, and play/pause on the lock screen.
- **Free use with no ads or purchases.** The app is [open source on GitHub](https://github.com/BibleGarden/).

Our library is small: English, Russian, and Ukrainian, with a few translations in each. We hand-pick each translation and recording for the quality of the text and sound. That focus is useful if you like to compare a carefully chosen pair as you listen; YouVersion is better when sheer breadth matters.

Bible Garden does not currently have an Android version, offline audio downloads, reading plans, search, or notes. An Android version and listening on the website are in our plans. ## What should you choose?

- **For the most translations, languages, and reading plans:** YouVersion. It is the strongest all-around library and the most popular app here.
- **For offline audio:** Bible.is or Dwell; Dwell's full library requires a subscription.
- **For a listening-first experience with voices and music:** Dwell. **For dramatized Scripture in many languages:** Bible.is.
- **For daily Catholic readings with commentary:** *The Bible in a Year* podcast.
- **For audio in a browser without installing an app:** Bible Gateway.
- **For thoughtful pauses or hearing the same passage in two translations or languages:** Bible Garden. See [how to read the Bible in two languages](/articles/bible-in-two-languages/).

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### What is the best free audio Bible app?

It depends on what you want to hear. YouVersion has the widest choice of translations and plans; Bible.is is free with downloadable and dramatized audio; Bible Garden is free and ad-free, with pauses and Multi Reading. Dwell has a limited free tier and charges a subscription for full access.

### Is there a free app that can read the entire Bible?

Yes. YouVersion and Bible.is offer complete audio Bibles in selected translations. Bible Garden has recordings of all 66 books in the Berean Standard Bible (Bob Souer or David) and the World English Bible, US edition (Winfred Henson). Its audio needs an internet connection.

### Is there a Bible app that reads to you with a voice-over?

Yes. All the apps compared here play spoken Scripture, though voices and available books vary by translation. Dwell lets you choose among 20+ voices; Bible.is offers dramatized recordings; Bible Garden lets you preview and select narrators for some translations.

### How can I listen to the Bible for free?

Install YouVersion or Bible.is, use Bible Gateway in a browser, or listen in Bible Garden on an iPhone or iPad. If you want a daily guided reading instead of selecting chapters yourself, subscribe to the free *Bible in a Year* podcast.

### Is there a Bible app without ads?

Yes. YouVersion, Bible.is, and Bible Garden are free and do not show ads. Dwell's full access requires a subscription. Bible Gateway's free service has ads, while Bible Gateway Plus removes them.
