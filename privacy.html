Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Overview

Bible Garden is a free, open-source application for reading and listening to the Bible. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping things simple: we do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No accounts or registration required
  • No personal information (name, email, phone)
  • No location data
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising
  • No cookies

Server Logs

When the app communicates with our server to load Bible texts and audio, we temporarily log basic request information (IP address, requested endpoint, timestamp) for operational purposes such as monitoring server health and preventing abuse. These logs are automatically deleted after 14 days and are never shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

Bible Garden does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app communicates only with our own server (api.bible.garden) to retrieve Bible content and audio files.

Website Analytics

The bible.garden website (not the app) counts visits with Umami, open-source analytics that runs on our own server, stats.bible.garden. Umami sets no cookies and does not store IP addresses: an address is used only while a visit is being processed, to derive an anonymous visitor identifier that changes every month; the country is taken from Cloudflare. What is stored: the page address without query string or fragment, the page title, the referring site (its address without query string), browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language, country and the time of the visit, plus clicks on the App Store link. The statistics are kept on servers under our control (the main server and its backups) and are not used for advertising or profiling. Records older than 13 months are deleted automatically every day; backup copies hold them for at most about a week longer. Cloudflare processes requests to stats.bible.garden as CDN and proxy, like the rest of the site, and receives no stored statistics from us.

Open Source

Bible Garden is fully open source. You can review the complete source code on GitHub to verify these privacy practices yourself.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update this page with the new date. Given the nature of our app, we do not anticipate significant changes.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, feel free to reach out via Telegram or GitHub.
